Terms and Conditions

  1. Fair play: Play honestly. Do not cheat, exploit bugs, or manipulate the game.
  2. Cards: Each active player receives three hidden cards per round, with a higher chance of ATTACK and BLANK. Flip only one card; the other two are discarded. Your chosen card is your only action for the round.
  3. Card effects: ATTACK targets another player; SHIELD blocks 1 damage point from incoming attacks; against a DOUBLE ATTACK, it blocks only 1 of the 2 damage points; REVERSE reflects one incoming attack; HEAL restores one life; JOKER activates a special effect. If it is CARD LOCK, the Joker owner chooses the opponent to block; BLANK has no effect.
  4. Lives: Everyone starts with three lives. At zero lives, the player is eliminated and cannot join later rounds.
  5. Host: The host starts the game, opens rounds, begins defense, resolves results, and may reset the game. The host also plays under the same rules.
  6. Respect: Treat all players respectfully. No bullying, threats, or harassment.
  7. Technical issues: Report problems to the host. The host may pause or reset the game if needed.
  8. Agreement: Accept these terms before entering the game. Violations may result in removal from the room.
